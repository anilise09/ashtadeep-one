The apps

Fourteen apps. One workspace.

Open a file and the right app opens it. Every app shares the same files, the same search and the same shortcuts.

  • One FilesManage every file in the workspace: folders, uploads, trash and search.
  • One PDFRead, annotate, sign, fill, organize and edit PDFs.
  • One WriteA full word processor with DOCX in and out.
  • One PadA fast, simple rich-text editor that starts instantly.
  • One TextCode and plain-text editor with 80+ languages.
  • One SheetSpreadsheets with formulas, charts and XLSX support.
  • One SlidesPresentations with themes, presenter view and PPTX export.
  • One DBA desktop-style relational database built on SQLite.
  • One WebBuild a website or blog and export it as a static site.
  • One ScanTurn camera photos into clean, searchable PDFs.
  • One PrintOne print center for every file type.
  • One FaxCompose and track faxes (demo provider until a fax service is connected).
  • One PaintA layered raster image editor.
  • One ViewImage viewer and organizer.

One PDF

Mark up, fill in and rearrange PDFs.

  • Highlight, underline, draw, add notes, shapes and stamps
  • Fill in forms and sign with a drawn, typed or uploaded signature
  • Reorder, rotate, split, merge and extract pages
  • Turn pages into pictures, and pictures into a PDF

One Write

A full word processor that speaks DOCX.

  • Styles, lists, tables, pictures, links and a table of contents
  • Opens and saves DOCX, HTML, Markdown, TXT and RTF
  • Letter, resume, report and memo templates
  • Find and replace, spell check, focus mode and page setup
More about One Write

One Sheet

Formulas, charts and XLSX.

  • 419 functions, with autocomplete in the formula bar
  • Charts, sorting, filters, frozen panes and merged cells
  • Several sheets per workbook, and room for a million rows
  • Opens and saves XLSX, CSV and TSV
More about One Sheet

One Slides

  • Exports PPTX, and each slide as a picture

Works together

One app hands off to the next.

No exporting and importing between programs. Each of these is tested from start to finish.

  • Write a letter in One Write, then print it from One Print.

  • Scan a page with One Scan, keep it in One Files, mark it up in One PDF.

  • Find a picture in One View, then edit it in One Paint.

  • Send a report from One DB or a deck from One Slides to one print center.

Private by design

Your files stay yours.

  • On your device

    Files are kept in your browser's own storage on this device. Nothing is uploaded.

  • Works offline

    Once installed, every app opens and makes its documents with no connection.

  • No tracking

    No analytics, no ads and no account. Nothing about you leaves the device.

How privacy works

Light by day. Dark by night.

It follows your device, or you choose. Every app is checked for readable contrast in both.

Straight answers.

Can I use Ashtadeep One now?

Not yet. Ashtadeep One is in development and there is nothing to download. The first fourteen apps work and are tested; six more are being built.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-in and no account. Your files stay on your device.

What will it cost?

That is not decided. Nothing is for sale yet.

Which devices does it run on?

Ashtadeep One is an app you install from your browser. So far it is tested in Chromium-based browsers on a computer. Other browsers, phones and tablets will be named here once they are tested. Desktop and mobile apps are planned.
